# Privacy Policy for Sudoku

**Effective Date:** July 2026

Sudoku is an open-source number puzzle game. We value your privacy and have designed the application to collect **no personal data**.

## 1. No Personal Data Collection
Sudoku does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, including but not limited to:
- Names, email addresses, or accounts
- IP addresses or device identifiers
- Behavioral analytics or tracking
- Geolocation data

## 2. Game Progress & Stats Stay on Your Device
The game records only your own **progress and gameplay stats** — such as levels completed, number of games finished, best times, and your settings (music, vibration, language). This information:
- Is stored **locally on your device** only,
- Is used solely to **show it back to you** inside the app,
- Is **never sent to any server**, and
- Is **never shared with anyone** — not with us, not with third parties, ever.

Uninstalling the app removes this data from your device.

## 3. No Payments
Sudoku contains no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or payments of any kind. We never ask for or process payment information.

## 4. No Ads or Third-Party Tracking
Sudoku does not contain advertisements, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics scripts (such as Google Analytics).

## 5. GDPR and CCPA Compliance
Since Sudoku does not process, store, or share any personal data, it is inherently compliant with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). We have no user data to share, sell, or lose.

## 6. Open Source Transparency
The source code for Sudoku is available for public inspection at: https://github.com/abhiabhi94/sudoku. You are welcome to review the code to verify these privacy claims.

## 7. Contact
If you have any questions or concerns regarding this policy, please open an issue on our official GitHub repository.
